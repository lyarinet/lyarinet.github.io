Privacy Policy for Shadow Switch

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Thank you for playing Shadow Switch ("we", "our", or "the Game"). Your privacy is paramount to us. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you download and play our mobile game.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Shadow Switch is an offline-first game. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII), such as your real name, email address, phone number, physical address, or biometric data.

  • No account registration or social login is required to play the game.
  • All game progress, level unlocks, coins, shadow crystals, and settings are stored locally on your device using encrypted local storage (SharedPreferences).

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

For free-tier players, the Game displays advertisements to support ongoing development. These advertisements are powered by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK).

Third-party advertising partners may collect anonymized device identifiers and diagnostic data (such as advertising ID, crash logs, and coarse network information) to serve personalized or non-personalized ads in compliance with Google Play Policies.

Learn more about how Google uses data: Google Privacy & Terms .

3. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Shadow Switch offers optional digital purchases (such as "Remove Ads / Lifetime Offline Access"). All transactions and financial information are processed securely by Google Play Billing. We do not collect, process, or store your credit card numbers, bank details, or billing address.

4. Network Access & Permissions

The Game requests standard Android permissions necessary for gameplay features:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required for free players to load ads and check active connectivity. (Paid users can play 100% offline).
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: Required to process in-app purchases securely via Google Play.
  • MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS: Required for low-latency game sound effects and hardware media volume routing.

5. Children’s Privacy

Shadow Switch does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Our advertisements are configured to adhere to Google Play Families Policy guidelines.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
